About

A short text block to frame how your engineering work, fashion practice and photography influence each other.

Skanda is a multidisciplinary designer and engineering student based in Italy, working across motorsport simulation, sportswear design and image-making.

This portfolio is a lightweight GitHub-hosted site designed around four themes: architecture, motorsport, astro and fashion. It is intentionally minimal so the images carry most of the voice.

As your projects evolve, you can branch this repo and grow each theme into deeper series — race analysis notes, garment specs, or written stories alongside the images.

Focus areas
  • Architecture — structural rhythm, facades, and city atmospheres.
  • Motorsport — track-side photography aligned with your vehicle dynamics work.
  • Astro — long-exposure experiments treating the sky like simulation data.
  • Fashion — performance, sportswear and editorial moments in motion.
